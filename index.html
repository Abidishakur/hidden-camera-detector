Room checklist

Check objects that have an unusual view of private areas.

1. Lens inspection helper

Start your phone camera to inspect objects closely. Move slowly and look for tiny glass-like reflections. Reflections can also come from ordinary screws, LEDs and shiny surfaces.

2. Check your Wi-Fi devices

This web app cannot scan your local network directly. Open your router's official app or admin page and review connected devices. Unknown names are not automatically cameras.

  • Check the device list in your router app.
  • Look for unfamiliar devices and compare their MAC addresses with devices you own.
  • Change your Wi-Fi password if you suspect unauthorized access.
  • Reconnect only your trusted devices.

3. Room inspection checklist

  • Inspect unfamiliar objects facing beds, bathrooms or changing areas.
  • Look closely at smoke alarms, clocks, chargers and decorative items without dismantling electrical equipment.
  • Check for tiny openings or unexplained lenses.
  • Do not touch or damage an object you believe may be evidence.
Important: No phone app can reliably detect every hidden camera. Camera detection by Wi-Fi, magnetic fields or reflections can produce false positives and miss devices. If you find something suspicious, photograph its position and seek help from the property owner or police rather than tampering with it.
